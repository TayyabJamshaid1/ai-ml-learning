Must follow 5 Rules Before Solving Any DSA Problem
1. Read Twice: Read the problem carefully. Identify the input, output, and constraints.

2. Understand the Problem: Explain in your own words what the problem is asking you to find or return.

3. Make an Example: Take a small example and manually calculate the expected output.

4. Think of Brute Force: First, think about how you would solve the problem manually without worrying about optimization.

5. Plan Before Coding: Decide the steps of your solution and choose the appropriate data structure or algorithm. Then start coding.

Remember: Understand → Example → Brute Force → Optimize → Code.
