Middle of linked list:
See Problem image
a)If size is odd number,then return the middle number e.g size 5 middle is 3,size 7 middle is 4
b)If size is even number,then 2 middle values is formed,we will consider the right side of middle value,e.g for size 8,middle is 4,5 so we will consider 5 as a middle number


Approach 1:(See Approach 1.png )

a)Convert the linked list into array
b) now divide the whole length with 2,middle will come

return arr[arr.length/2]

It is not a good approach for interviewers because this approach will use extra memory with space complexity of O(n) and time complexity will also O(n)

Approach 2: (See approach 2.png)
We will use slow & fast pointer approach 
This approach says i will keep 2 pointers
 Slow pointer---1 step ahead
 Fast pointer---2 step ahead
 i will move one by one but slow pointer moves 1 step at a time and fast pointer moves 2 step at a time.I will keep doing this and once i reach end of the list for the fast pointer,we have to stop the loop ,at that time slow pointer will be at a middle point.Both pointers will start from head.
 
 Imp: We have to make sure where the fast pointer ends
 
 Code:
 
 function MiddleNode(head){
 let fast=head,slow=head;
 
 while(fast && fast.next){
 slow=slow.next;
 fast=fast.next.next
 }
 return slow
 }
