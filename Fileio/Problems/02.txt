Problem:WAF to find in which line of the file does the word “learning”occur first

Solution 1:
word="learning"
with open("practice.txt","r") as f:
     no_of_line=1
     findLine=True

     while(findLine):
           readData=f.readline()
           if (word in readData):
               print(f"line find {no_of_line}")
               findLine=False
           no_of_line+=1
